Online Intake

Ready to get started?

Complete the intake form below and we'll be in touch within 1–2 business days. Your information is kept private and confidential.

Have these ready before you begin

  • Medicaid ID & health plan (MCO) name
  • Front & back of your insurance card
  • Emergency contact name & phone
  • Parent/guardian info, if applicable
  • Current medications & prescribers
  • Primary care provider name & phone

Answer what you can. Write N/A when a question doesn't apply. Some questions are personal; you can choose “Prefer to discuss” and we'll talk about it together.

If you are having thoughts of harming yourself or someone else right now, please don't wait for us to contact you. Call or text 988, or call 911.

Having trouble with the form? Open it in a new tab or contact us.
Prefer paper? Download the printable intake (PDF) and bring it to your first appointment.
