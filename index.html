About Us

Support that meets you where you are

At Share Self Care LLC, we believe everyone deserves someone in their corner. Life can feel overwhelming when you're juggling work, school, housing, and your own well-being. You don't have to figure it out alone.

We work alongside you to set realistic goals, build daily habits that stick, and connect you with the right community resources. Every plan is built around you: your strengths, your pace, and what matters most in your life.

What We Offer

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for services?

Anyone with active North Carolina Medicaid coverage may qualify. If you're unsure about your coverage, contact us and we can help you find out.

Is there a cost?

Services are covered through Medicaid, so there is no out-of-pocket cost for eligible members.

Is this therapy?

No. We provide encouragement, skill-building, and practical support. If you'd like therapy, we can refer you to licensed mental health providers.

How do sessions take place?

Support may be offered in person, by phone, or virtually depending on your needs and location. We'll discuss what works best for you.

How soon will I hear back after completing the intake?

We typically respond within 1–2 business days to schedule your first conversation.

Can a parent or guardian complete the intake?

Yes. If a parent, guardian, or legally responsible person (LRP) is involved, include their information in the intake and they can help complete it.

What happens after I submit the intake?

We review your answers, verify your Medicaid coverage, and reach out to schedule your first conversation. Additional assessment and consent forms may be needed before services begin. We'll walk you through them.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your information is confidential and only used to provide and coordinate your services.
